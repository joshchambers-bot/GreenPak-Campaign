What GreenPAK Replaces

Most boards carry a scatter of small parts that hold everything together: gates, flip-flops, timers, comparators, reset supervisors, level shifters, and the passives around them. GreenPAK folds those into one chip you configure yourself.

TodayDiscrete glue logic

  • Separate chips for logic, delays, sequencing, and supervision
  • More placements, more routing, more board area
  • Every design change can mean a new part or a board respin
  • Off-the-shelf parts that anyone can read off your board

With GreenPAKOne configurable chip

  • Logic, timing, analog, and power functions in one package
  • Fewer placements and a smaller footprint
  • Change the circuit in software and re-sample — no new part
  • A custom part number and datasheet for your design

When It's the Right Call — and When It Isn't

GreenPAK pays off when it consolidates several functions. It isn't the answer to every part on your BOM.

Good Fit

  • Your board has several small logic, timing, or supervision parts you could merge
  • Board space, part count, or standby current is tight
  • You expect the circuit to change late in the design
  • You want a custom part that's harder to copy off the board

Probably Not

  • You need one simple gate — a single discrete part is likely cheaper
  • The function needs firmware — GreenPAK is configured hardware, so it sits next to a microcontroller rather than replacing one
